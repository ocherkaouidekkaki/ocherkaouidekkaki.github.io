06 / BACKGROUND

A mathematical lens. A systems perspective.

My research began with bioeconomic modeling and resource-management problems before extending to control-theoretic questions involving nonlinear systems, constrained trajectories, robust invariance, and their applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Mohammed V University in 2024 and completed postdoctoral research at the College of Computing, Mohammed VI Polytechnic University, on correct-by-design control of microgrids.
